# Authorship

**Authors:** S. Uskov, A. Chervov, F. Levkovich-Maslyuk, I. Litvak, A. Smolensky.

S. Uskov: [GitHub](https://github.com/snow-ghost),
[ORCID 0009-0006-5845-7013](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-5845-7013).

The project contains a proof and Lean 4 formalization of the exact directed
L/X diameter formula for the symmetric groups S_n, for all n ≥ 5.
The proof and formalization were developed with assistance from
OpenAI Codex. The mathematical claims are stated explicitly in the Lean source;
reproducible verification instructions are in the README.

The underlying problem and earlier computations predate this project.
See [OEIS A039745](https://oeis.org/A039745), credited there to David desJardins,
and its references to the work of A. Chervov, D. Fedoriaka, et al.
The repository uses Lean 4 and the Mathlib library; their authors retain credit
for those tools and dependencies.

Use `CITATION.cff` to cite this proof and formalization. The project is licensed
under [MIT](LICENSE).
